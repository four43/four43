Gearbox

Keys: W/S or arrows to drive and brake, A/D to steer, Space for the handbrake, Q/E to shift, R to get back onto the road, Shift+R (or hold Reset) to restart, Esc or P to pause, C for camera. A gamepad works too: triggers, left stick, A for the handbrake, bumpers to shift, Start to pause. In menus the arrow keys, D-pad or stick move, Enter or A selects, Esc or B goes back, and the bumpers change the highlighted option.

Reverse: in Auto, hold brake when stopped; in Manual, shift down from 1st.
