# Tractor Pickup reading script

Record everything below in **one take**: phone voice memo, quiet room (a closet full of clothes is perfect), phone about 15 cm from your mouth.
Leave **one full second of silence between items**. Mess up? Pause, then say it again: the last take of each word wins.
Drop the recording (any format) into `audio/voice/` and ask Claude to slice it.

**How to say the words:** level and even, like reading items off a list, because the game joins words into sentences ("three … plus … two … makes … five"). Only the cheers in part 4 should be big.

---

## Part 1: Numbers

Read slowly, one per breath:

zero · one · two · three · four · five · six · seven · eight · nine · ten · eleven · twelve

## Part 2: Animals

pig · cow · chicken · sheep · duck · bunny · dog · chick · golden

## Part 3: More than one

pigs · cows · chickens · ducks · bunnies · dogs · chicks

## Part 4: Phrases

Say each line as written. Where a line has "…", that is a pause: the game cuts the line there into two clips.

1. Let's find … animals!
2. Great job!
3. Go to the barn!
4. Let's count!
5. Hooray!
6. You did it!
7. You got a sticker!
8. plus
9. makes
10. The animals are sleepy … Goodnight!
11. Wake up!

## Part 5: Animal sounds

For each animal, say its **name**, wait one second, then make its **sound** (one or two calls), then wait two seconds.
Keep the sounds short (under about 1 second): they play when the horn honks and the riders sing along.

| Say | Then make the sound |
|---|---|
| pig | oink oink |
| cow | moooo |
| chicken | bawk bawk |
| sheep | baaa |
| duck | quack quack |
| bunny | a little squeak or sniff-sniff |
| dog | woof woof |
| chick | peep peep |

Optional: do part 5 a second time with different calls. The game picks one at random each time.
